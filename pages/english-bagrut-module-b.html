THE WALKING BUS

I   On school mornings, the streets near many schools are full of cars. Most parents drive their children to school because they think it is the fastest and safest way. But hundreds of cars arriving together cause traffic jams and air pollution near the school gates. Doctors are worried too. They say that children today spend too many hours sitting, and that a short walk in the morning is good exercise that helps children stay healthy and learn better.

II   Two years ago, Helen Carter, a retired nurse from Bristol, England, walked past a school near her home. She saw a long line of cars next to the school gate. "The children sat in the cars and looked bored and tired," says Helen. "When I was a girl, I walked to school with my friends every day, and I loved it. So I decided to start a walking bus."

III   A walking bus is a group of children who walk to school together with two adults. One adult walks at the front of the group and is called the "driver". The other adult walks at the back and is called the "conductor". The children wait at special "bus stops" along the route*, and the walking bus picks them up on its way to school. Everyone in the group wears a yellow vest*, so car drivers can see the group easily.

IV   Helen's first walking bus had only six children. Today there are eleven walking buses in her neighborhood, and more than 150 children walk to school with them every morning. Teachers say that the children arrive at school happy and full of energy. But running the walking buses is not always easy. "The hardest part is finding enough adults to walk with the groups," Helen explains. "Most parents leave for work very early, so we need more volunteers*."

V   Helen believes that walking buses do much more than bring children to school. "Children who walk together talk, laugh and make new friends," she says. "The streets near the school are quieter now, and the air is cleaner. Best of all, the children get exercise every day without even thinking about it. I hope that one day every school will have a walking bus."

* route מסלול / مسار

* vest אפוד / سترة

* volunteers מתנדבים / متطوّعون

It starts with the cars outside the school and ends with what doctors say about walking in the morning. Cross out any option that needs information the paragraph never gives - other countries, how many cars a family has, school opening times. שאלת רעיון מרכזי על פסקה I: קוראים את הפסקה עד סופה ופוסלים כל אפשרות שנשענת על מידע שלא נמצא בה.

iii. Check: "Everyone in the group wears a yellow vest, so car drivers can see the group easily." The other reasons are not mentioned in the text. (הסיבה היחידה שמצוינת היא בטיחותית: שנהגים יראו את הקבוצה.)
